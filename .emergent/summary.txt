<product_requirements>
The user requested an Android mobile app for internal audits, specifically ISO 45001:2018 standards for packaged drinking water plants, later expanded to include ISO 9001:2015 and ISO 14001:2015. Core functionalities include: editable questions (add, edit, delete, reorder), comprehensive evidence attachment support (photos, documents, audio, video), user authentication, pre-populated questionnaires, audit creation and management, recording observations and conformance (initially Meets, Minor, Major; later standardized to C, Mi, Ma), PDF/text download of audit reports (including findings summary), capturing audit parameters (plant name, auditor/auditee name, scope, audit criteria), deleting draft audits, and logout. Recent additions include customizable app name ('GO AUDIT'), developer credits, and initial implementation for CAPA reporting and auditor qualifications.
</product_requirements>
